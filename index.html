Cara Kerja Adimology
1. Hitung Fraksi
Jumlah tick harga antara ARB dan ARA berdasarkan aturan fraksi IDX (Rp1/2/5/10/25)
2. Avg Bid/Offer
Rata-rata volume bid+offer per tick = (Total Bid + Total Offer) / Total Fraksi
3. Power Fraksi
Berapa tick broker bisa gerakkan harga = Buy Lot / Avg Bid Offer per tick
4. Target Realistis
Target 5% + power fraksi ticks. Low = ½ power, High = full power
